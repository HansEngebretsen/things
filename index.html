Some things I've made, by Hans Engebretsen

I make things that help people spend more time on what matters. I've loved getting to do that in different ways the last 20 years as a designer, front-end developer, Uxer, graphic artist, art director, farmhand, newspaper boy, and press operator.

At the moment, I lead design for agentic platforms on security products at Google. In the past, I've worked on developer tools, Auth, consumer electronics, and government/philanthropy. I've helped build and launch 20+ products - some flopped, but some grew to hundreds of millions in revenue & have reached billions of people. There's just-for-fun work below, reach out for the business stuff.

  • Moods: A mood ring for my speakers: one tap to just play something on Sonos, then nudge the volume or the rooms.
  • canvas: An infinite canvas for live websites and HTML.
  • quadrant: A quick 2×2 matrix for sorting ideas.
  • batchicons: Pick a whole set of Material icons in a few clicks and take them all with you: one copy for every SVG, or one download as a zip.
  • loupe: Magnify the detail that matters in any screenshot.
  • peeps: Illustrated personas for slides and docs.
  • md: A dead simple markdown editor.
  • bingo: Halloween bingo for kids and spooky movie nights.
  • score: A scorekeeper for game night, built for your phone.
  • folio: A dense, single-file portfolio forecaster.
  • firecalc: A FIRE calculator you play with rather than fill out.
  • made: It's surprisingly hard to see what a salary pays you for one day of work.
  • calc: A capital gains calculator for one stock sale.
  • krakentix: Five seasons of Seattle Kraken season tickets, every seat I kept or resold, laid out so you can see in one glance what the hockey actually cost.
  • scoreboard: Scores, standings and the next game for every team I follow across MLB, NHL, NBA and NFL, on one board you can read at a glance.
  • odds: Kraken playoff odds you can play with.
  • hanstaxi: When's the next West Seattle water taxi? The next sailing each way, a countdown to departure, and where the boat is right now.
  • fishreports: Puget Sound salmon, month by month: recreational catch and angler trips in Marine Areas 10 and 11 against fish passage counts, from public WDFW, Army Corps and tribal data.
  • japanclan: Organize travel details.
  • cntrl: Run a TRMNL e-ink display from your phone.
