Colour for Calm Country

Every mood has its own colour, and each song nudges it a little, so a playlist drifts gently around its mood's colour. Swatches recolour the mood that's playing. Text style is set per theme (Dark and Light each remember their own); both start on Gradient. Palettes on top, text treatments below. Tinted pulls the palette into the text and makes chips translucent. Gradient paints the title and play button with the palette. Reverse inverts the text against whatever is behind it. Knockout turns the card into a block of colour with the text reversed out, like print. Tap any card to put it on the widget.
